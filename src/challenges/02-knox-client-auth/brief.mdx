## Scenario

Knox works the front desk at Nine Lives and loves locks. He labels cabinets,
checks the deadbolt twice, and built the staff login page with the confidence of
someone who has never watched a browser run JavaScript line by line.

The page looks convincing: username field, password field, denial message,
staff-only area. Volunteers see only a polite rejection and a reminder that
donation receipts live somewhere else. Knox says the important part is that the
form "knows" the correct credentials and only opens when they match.

Your job is to review that claim from inside the browser. The target is fully
self-contained, and the staff area is already part of the mini-application. You
can use the page, the devtools console, or the source visible to the browser.
Nothing you do leaves the sandbox.

Win condition: reach the staff-only area and recover the flag it displays. If
you find more than one way through, keep track of them. Knox needs to understand
that the problem is the whole trust model, not a single misplaced string.
