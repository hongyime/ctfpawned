## Scenario

Scrambles is proud of the Nine Lives member portal because the session preview
looks official and the little padlock icon in the corner makes everyone stop
asking questions. The portal lets volunteers check their own profile, preferred
shift, and current access level before they arrive at the shelter. Scrambles
calls the session blob "scrambled text" and insists that nobody can read it
unless they are supposed to.

Your audit account is ordinary. It can see the volunteer welcome panel, the
orientation notes, and a very cheerful message about not touching the staff
drawer. The staff panel is present in the same target, but it only appears when
the session says the visitor has staff access.

The target gives you the current session value and a way to paste an updated
one back into the portal. You are not trying to guess a password or attack a
real service. You are checking whether the browser-held session actually proves
what Scrambles thinks it proves.

Win condition: make the staff panel render inside the target and recover the
flag shown there.
