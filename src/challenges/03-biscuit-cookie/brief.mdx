## Scenario

Biscuit runs the treat-ordering kiosk, which is the most politically sensitive
system at Nine Lives. Staff can order the good treats. Volunteers can order the
training treats. Visitors can browse, but not submit anything, after last year's
catnip incident.

The kiosk remembers who gets the staff menu by reading a small bit of browser
state. Biscuit likes this arrangement because it feels simple: the browser
brings a label, the page reads the label, and the right menu appears. She has
not explained why the browser should be trusted to label itself.

Your audit account starts on the volunteer menu. The staff order panel is in
the same target, but it does not render until the page believes the visitor is
staff. Since the target runs in a sandboxed frame, its browser state is shimmed
for the exercise and will not appear in the usual Application panel.

Win condition: make the staff treat-ordering panel appear inside the target and
recover the flag generated from the state that unlocked it.
